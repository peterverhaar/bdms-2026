Dear Mr. Woolf:
(tab*4?) I recieved yesterday your cable reading:
"Accept Atlantic Hogarth." Upon recieveng it, I telephoned 
to Boston and talked with Mr. Sedgwick and succeded in
persuading him to push the price up to (dollar)1500. The sale of
the serial rights is therefore concluded at that figure.
I am sure you won't mind the additional sum, and the price 
of (dollar)1500 seems more appropriate than the (dollar)1200 he offered.

(tab*4?) I assume that you have gone ahead to set up
the book by this time, and I am hoping that we may receive 
corrected proofs before long. It would be well to send us
two sets, one of which I might send on to the Atlantic.
No doubt, it will be better for them to print from the 
corrected version if possible. Also, although book pub-
lication is put off until October, we shall still want as 
complete samples as possible early in June. I should be
glad, therefore, if we might have the photographs you 
select as soon as possible, as well as the corrected proofs:
or if the corrected proofs are to be long in coming, at 
least the photographs.
(right)
With kind regards to you both,
Yours sincerely,
(signature)Donald Brace
