My Introduction

by Justin van den Oever

Hello everybody, my name is Justin van den Oever and I am 22 years old. I was born in Leiden and currently live with my parents, two brothers and a cat in the nearby village of Valkenburg.

Undergraduate Studies

Before joining the MA Book and Digital Media Studies programme, I completed my Bachelor's degree in Archaeology at Leiden University.

Why BDMS

I am interested in history and during my previous Archaeology studies, I specialized in material culture. I was able to study various types of objects, unfortunately books were not among them. That is why I chose the BDMS program, to learn about the history and material aspects of books. Additionally, I want to broaden my horizons regarding books and publishing in te digital age with an eye toward potential future career opportunities.

Hobbies

I enjoy immersing myself in stories, whether through books, movies, TV series or video games. I'm especially drawn to thrilling action-adventures and mysteries. I also enjoy solving puzzles and playing card or board games.
