about me

by tess lewis

I grew up in san francisco, california so I'll always be most at home right by the beach! I moved to portland, oregon for university at reed college. I studied history and wrote my thesis on small scale organic farming in the pacific northwest. after college I worked on different farms around california and oregon growing vegetables and flowers before moving here to nl.

in my free time I love to go camping, hiking and doing all kinds of outdoor activities, but my biggest hobby is reading. my favorite genres are science fiction and fantasy.

I could never decide on my favorite books, but here are some of my top reads lately:
