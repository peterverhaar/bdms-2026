Hello! I'm Cristi, I'm 22 and I'm from Scotland. Last month, I moved to Den Haag, in order to study the Book and Digital Media Masters at Leiden University along with all of you!

Previous Education:

Last year, I graduated from my Bachelor's degree in English Literature from the University of Edinburgh, which I personally think was a great place to study that course. It's a beautiful city, and one that I feel so lucky to have grown up close to - it was also the first place to be named a UNESCO city of literature for its history of producing renowned writers, and its thriving contemporary book scene (bookstores, festivals, authors coming to visit … there's a lot going on). The course gave me a range of varied and interesting modules - The Making of Modern Fantasy, Psychoanalysis, The Bible in Literature, to name a few - but it was 19th century literature in particular that the degree sparked my love for. In my final year, I wrote my dissertation on representations of the 'drowned woman' in English and North American novels and poetry of the 19th century, and am still thoroughly fascinated by reading books from that time.

Other interests:

Unsurprisingly, I love to read; besides the classics, I also like fantasy, magical-realism, and the occasional book of poetry. When I find the time, I also like to be creative - painting and drawing primarily - usually nature scenes or fun trees I run across. One of my proudest creative achievements was designing the magazine for the creative writing magazine I was part of at Edinburgh University, where I got to make art to go along with the pieces, and combine my two favourite things - art and reading - into one.
