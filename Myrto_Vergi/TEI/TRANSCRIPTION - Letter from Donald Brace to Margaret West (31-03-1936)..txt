                                                                  March 31, 1936.
Miss Margaret West
The Hogarth Press
52 Tavistook Square
London, W.C.1

Dear Miss West:
               Many thanks for your letters regarding Mrs. Woolf's new book.
I am especially delighted that you are sending the proofs in installments
and without waiting for the corrections. The proofs will be useful in all
sorts of ways, though I expect we shall wait for a corrected set before
setting. It usually works out that bi time is lost by waiting for corrected
proofs, and of course expense is saved. I am not going to be able to resist
reading the installments as they come in; I have just read the first twelve 
galleys, and I am watching every mail for more. 
                
              Your letter of February tenth said that you hoped to have the
manuscript ready for the printer about the middle of March and to dispatch 
us a corrected proof from which to set about the end of April. The arrival
of these first proofs seems to indicate that you are so far somewhat ahead
of this tentative schedule. You also said in your letter of February tenth 
that you were hoping to publish the book about the middle of May. I don't 
know what can be done about it, but according to this tentative plan, we
should be receiving the finally corrected proof only a few days before your
date of publication. Evidently we are going to have a difficult decision f 
to make. If we hurry up and publish as promptly as we can, we are going to 
be very close to the beginning of summer, which is probably not the best
time to publish, and we shall have less time than I should like for useful
advance work. Alternatively, we could put off publication here until early
autumn, or at any rate until August; but I dislike having so long an inter-
val, three months or more, between English and American publication. A good 
many readers will be unwilling to wait so long for this new book, and there 
is no way to prevent a considerable importation (?) of your edition. I'd be 
glad if you would mention this problem to Mr. and Mrs. Woolf and if they
would give such consideration to it as they can. The ideal, of course, 
would be simultaneous publication. And any extent to which you feel able
to wait for us to catch up would be helpful. 
               
                Will you let me know whether you plan to use a wrapper by
Vanessa Bell? If no, how soon may we see proof of it?

                The most recent photograph we have of Mrs. Woolf has been used
with FLUSH and THE WAVES. A never one would be valuable for us. 

                                            Yours sincerely, 
                                           (signature Donald Brace)
