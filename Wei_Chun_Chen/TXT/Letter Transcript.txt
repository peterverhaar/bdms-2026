21 B Regents Park Road
N.W.1
Dec. 4th 1960

Dear Mr. Woolf
I have just finished reading with very great
pleasure your book "Sow-ing", which I have
had from the library; and I am now going to buy it.
I cant resist telling you how much I have enjoyed
it, and how I hope the next volumes are going
to come along quickly. So interesting, so readable,
so wise, and so sensible : I have enjoyed every page
of it. Although it is about times and places
unknown to me, I found it extremely convincing —
so many biographies build up a personality
that does not seem like that of the person one has
met.
I dont want to put you to the trouble
of replying to this but you may be interested
briefly to hear what has become of myself and
Margaret. Having been turned out under the
Rent Act, after a long search, we were at last able
to buy the freehold of a house along the road for
£5,500. We think we have done rather well as
prices particularly in this district have rocketed
in the eighteen months since then and this year
the adjoining house went for over £7000!
We live in the bottom part, which we knocked

[page 2]
about a bit to suit ourselves, with a small but
nice garden, and over our heads are three flats
let helping to pay off the mortgage, so we hope we
are doing something towards our unpensioned old
age.
Magaret is still in commercial teaching and starts
a new job at the Language Tuition Centre in Oxford Street
after Christmas. I am now senior art tutor at the
College at Barnet, which I still like very much.
I am hoping for another one-man show in the
Spring at "The Canaletto", a gallery, you may
have heard of, newly opened on a barge on the
canal near Paddington.
I am now exactly the age you were when
I first came to the Hogarth Press and that
must be a quarter of a century ago!
It is nice to see Hogarth Press imprint still on
good books and to think that you are still to
be found in Rodmell.

With good wishes
Yours ever
Barbara
