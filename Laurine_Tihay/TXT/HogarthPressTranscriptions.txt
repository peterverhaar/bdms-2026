----- Letter from Vanessa Bell to John Lehmann -----

Ap. 14

Dear John
Leonard asks me to return this directly to you & to tell you that it must be THE death of the Moth. Also that "and other essays" must be deleted. I prefer A to B. "Death of the Moth" must be lower, the bottom line of the letters to come where the smaller letters now come and THE must be put in the centre above in the smaller type.
Also, the name at the bottom I think should be a tiny bit lower, so as to be more on a lever with the drawing – I have marked the place in pencil.
I think it should be in the blackest ink on the whitest paper possible.

Your Vanessa

G.A. Baxter, Esq.,                                         
18th April, 1940

Abbey Street,
Melrose,
Roxburgshire.

Dear Sir,
Woolf – Common Reader, First Series

In reply to your letter of April 9th, we shall be glad to give you permission to use the passage you quote, i.e. from "Admitting the vagueness" down to "than custom would have us believe it," from the above book, on payment of a fee of one and a half guiness. Our minimum fee for extracts from Mrs. Woolf's works is usually a guinea a page so we hope that the reduced fee will be satisfactory to you. We take it the usual acknowledgements will be made.

Yours faithfully,
THE HOGARTH PRESS
P.T.O.

Manager.
