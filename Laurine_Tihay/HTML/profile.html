Hello everybody!

My name is Laurine and I am 30 years old. I grew up in eastern France, in a city near Switzerland called Besançon. After that, I lived near Paris for a while, then I moved to the Netherlands five years ago. It was not my decision (my husband had a professional opportunity), but I wouldn't leave for anything in the world!

Aside from reading (I’m passionate about social literature and crime fiction), I love green tea, cats and dogs, browsing through libraries for pleasure, traveling the world, being a homebody, using brackets and semicolon, and writing, especially detective novels. By the way, perhaps, and I do mean perhaps, I am a crime writer published in France (under a pseudonym). So, if you ever need to hide a body, don't hesitate to contact me—I know a thing or two about it... on paper.

My academic background/working experience

After completing a literature-focused baccalauréat, I studied English literature and French linguistics at Marie et Louis Pasteur University in Besançon. Upon graduating in 2017, I initially worked in a field completely unrelated to my main academic studies: I served as a support officer for victims of violence within the French National Police in Parisian suburbs, as I also have a certification in psychocriminology and victimology.

After some time, I quit to pursue a career in auditing, then in publishing, as a proofreader, typesetter and professional beta reader. I was almost exclusively working for publishers and authors of crime fiction. Then, after moving to the Netherlands, I worked as a linguistics content writer and editor for an international company specialized in producing online educational content based in Amsterdam... until today.

Why BDMS?

I am resuming my studies eight years after graduating in order to go back to work in the publishing world... or perhaps in cultural heritage—who knows? I am open to any opportunity, as long as it involves books and culture. And if it could be in the field of the humanities or social sciences, I would be the happiest person in the world!
