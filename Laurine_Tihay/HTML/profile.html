A few words about me

Academic background

I was born and raised in eastern France.

After completing a literature-focused high school diploma, I studied English literature and French linguistics at Marie et Louis Pasteur University in Besançon.

First steps into the working world, first experiences in the publishing field...

Upon graduating in 2017, I initially worked in a field completely unrelated to my main academic studies: I served as a support officer for victims of violence within the French National Police in Parisian suburbs, as I also have a certification in psychocriminology and victimology (I’ve always struggled to choose between my passion for books and linguistics and my passion for criminal sciences… so I decided not to choose and study both).

I eventually left the police after some time to pursue a career in auditing, then in publishing, as a proofreader, typesetter and professional beta reader. I was almost exclusively working for publishers and authors of crime fiction, my favorite literary genre (if you ever need to hide a body, don't hesitate to contact me—I know a thing or two about it... on paper.)

Settling to the Netherlands

I put this career paths on hold when I moved to the Netherlands in 2021 (not my decision, but I wouldn't leave for anything in the world!). Since then, I have worked as a linguistics content writer (mostly in French, a bit in English) for an international company specialized in producing online educational content based in Amsterdam.

I write about topics as varied as grammar, linguistics, lexicology, typography, literary genres and narratology, with the aim of making academic concepts accessible, even if it also involves understanding digital marketing in order to unlock the inner workings of search engines and trends.
