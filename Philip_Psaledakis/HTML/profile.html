Philip Psaledakis, A Short Introduction

by Philip Psaledakis

About Me

I grew up in the mountains of Colorado. Much of my time was spent in the outdoors, hiking, running, and cycling during the summers and skiing during the winters. After graduating high school, my play became my work. I joined research teams affiliated with both The Nature Conservancy and the United States Geological Survey conducting field studies in the Southeast Utah wilderness and national park system. Later, I worked as a sawyer with the United States Forest Service in the Manti-La Sal National Forest, also in Southeast Utah.

I have also alwyas been a bibliophile. If I wasn't outside, I was either at home rearranging my bookshelves or at the bookstore wandering the stacks. My love of reading, collecting, and handling books lead me to Santa Fe, New Mexico, where I attended St. John's College. St. John's is a unique institution in that it offers an all-required liberal arts education rooted in the study of primary sources across a wide variety of disciplines. The curriculum is organized chronologically, bringing students from the study of Ancient Greek authors in their Freshman year to contemporary authors in their Senior year.

I moved home after I graduated from St. John's and took a job at Explore Books, my childhood bookstore. My work here as both Lead Bookseller and Receiving Manager exposed me to both the front and back of house operations of the book trade, sparking my interest in pursuing a bookish career. What sort of career is still challenging to say, though I hope to discover that while moving through our MA program this year.
