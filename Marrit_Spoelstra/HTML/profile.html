About Me

Hey guys welcome to my page! My name is Marrit, I am 24 years old, and I am from the Netherlands. I currenly live in Leiden, but I am originally from Sneek, which is a city in the Dutch province of Friesland in the north of the Netherlands. I work in the public library in Friesland, and will soon start working in the public library in Leiden as well. In my free time I like reading books, watching movies (I also love going to the cinema), and I like to knit.

Undergrad

I started the Bachelor's programme for History here in Leiden in 2021, and I graduated last semester. During those five years, I did many things. I took a gap year after my second year, to work in Paris for a while as an au pair. During my third year, I did a minor programme in religious studies, and I extended my BA by doing a "fourth year" so I could go study abroad with Erasmus+! I went to Galway, Ireland, where I studied History at the University of Galway, which was amazing. I wrote my thesis about the role of women in the modern Dutch cremation movement of the late nineteenth century, and the early twentieth century- since they were actually very active!! Which has been largely ignored by Dutch historians of cremation :) When you click on the link you can find out more about the BA History at Leiden University.

BDMS

Prior Knowledge During my BA, I had to work with the archives quite a lot, both the digital archives and the physical ones, so I am quite familiar with those. However, the documents I used to work with were a lot less old than the ones we use have to analyse for this MA! I also took a class on early modern Dutch palaeography, which is somewhat useful for understanding medieval handwritings. Though, I am very unfamiliar with the work in the publishing field (and computer stuffs like what we are doing right now), so I am really curious to know more about that!
Expectations I hope to gain more knowledge about the ways of the publishing world, since I would love to work in that field some day. I also really hope that I will improve my encoding skills and whatnot, because I find it to be really difficult!
