An Introduction of Skye

by Skye

Hi everyone! My name is Skye Theo Rijsdijk. I am non-binary and use they/them pronouns. I am 22 years old, born on the 18th of December 2003 in Rotterdam, where I grew up. Two years ago I moved to Leiden to be closer to the university, and I love it here! Because I did a bachelor in Classics at Leiden University, which I finished this summer, I know a bit about Latin and Ancient Greek. I work at Bruna (a bookstore chain in The Netherlands) in Leiden, so do come by to buy books!

That's me on vacation in Girona a month ago!

In my free time I like to read books (obviously), listen to music, and take walks along the Witte Singel (the water you cross when you go from Lipsius to the University Library or the other way around). I've also recently taking up running as a hobby, and I occasionaly write a poem or a story.

I don't know enough languages to make a list, so here's my favourite books:

  • Don't Let the Forest In by CG Drews
  • The Song of Achilles by Madeline Miller
  • The Pairing by Casey McQuiston
  • Five Survive by Holly Jackson
  • The Sunbearer Trials by Aiden Thomas
  • Six of Crows by Leigh Bardugo
