11.11.24

Dear Mr. Woolf,
	I now return the contract
complete with my signature. Thank
you very much for the spare costs
of the type-script which I received this
morning.
	As regards the specimen page of
print which you sent me, I
wonder if the double line at the
top enclosing the title,
looks a little heavy? But
I wish to leave this entirely to
your judgment.

I am
yours sincerely
Clifford H. B. Kitchin

Leonard Woolf Esq.
