8th January 1945

Dear Mr. Lehmann
	You may remember we obtained your per-
mission on 30th November to broadcast an
extract of 1810 words from "I am Christina
Rossetti" by Virginia Woolf once throughout
our Overseas Service in English, the first
broadcast performance being 2nd December
1944.
	I now understand that this extract,
besides being broadcast once throughout our
Overseas Service in English, was repeated
in one of these transmissions on 4th January
this year. You are therefore entitled to
an additional fee of £4.18.Qd. for the
broadcasting of this extract once in one
transmission and I am therefore arranging
for the additional payment of £4.18.Qd. to
be made to you.

Yours sincerely
[author signature]
