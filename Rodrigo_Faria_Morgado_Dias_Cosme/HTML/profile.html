BA Digital Society

I completed my previous studies at Maastricht University in BA Digital Society. In short, it's an interdisciplinary programme in the field of sociology with a focus on how digitalisation and the rapidly advancing modern-day technologies may impact (whether positively or negatively) societal processes.

Book Recommendations

Fahrenheit 451 (1953)

by Ray Bradbury

I wasn't a big fan of the ending, but the book overall is a real eye-opener for fans of dystopian books. The story takes place in a reality where books have been banned, and it follows the life of a 'fireman' tasked with burning any and all books reported to the authorities.

Dungeon Crawler Carl (2020)

by Matt Dinniman

This one is incredibly well-written and definitely one of my favourite books. I know the cover looks goofy (and I definitely tend to judge books by their covers myself), but I gave it a try, and it was absolutely worth it. Also, one of the main characters is a cat, so.... cool :)

Current Read

Project Hail Mary (2021)

by Andy Weir

I finally caved under peer pressure and started reading this, but it's taking me unusually long. The plot is really interesting, but the writing is too technical. Still recommendable, though.
