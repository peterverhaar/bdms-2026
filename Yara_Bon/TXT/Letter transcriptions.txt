Dear Mr. Woolf,

    Thank you very much for your letter of Oct. 25th. I am glad to avail myself to your offer to publish my novel on the 'half-profit system', and will also give you the first refusal of my next book.
	I will send ou the revised type-script in two or three days, when I have made a few corrections whcih I have in mind.I shall also change the title, with which I have never been satisfied. 

Yours sincerely,
 Clifford H.B. Kitchin
	  
	  
Dear Gerry,
    I took the liberty of opening your letter to Ian as he is in Italy and hopes to be there fore another ten days or so. I know I am safe in sending you a copy of THE BODY with his love, but the V.W. is not yet ready and doesn't look like being so for a month or so. It will probably come in on the 24th of December. 
	Your name shall, however, be recorded among the faithful.
	
    I hope all goes well.

                   Yours ever,
