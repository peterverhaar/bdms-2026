Me, Myself and I

by Maria Vamvouka

Hi all! My name is Maria Vamvouka as some of you may already know, it has been really nice to meet you all. I am from Greece, specifically from the island of Crete, though I am also from a small town in Northern Greece. I completed my undergraduate studies in English Language and Literature, in Aristotle University of Thessaloniki (AUTH), and because of my extensive contact with different kinds of books and literature I became interested in the book as a historical entity and presence. While I am very interested in the process of publishing a book, I am equally fascinated by its history and development, and since I do not have much background on the subject from my previous studies, every class is something new that I get to discover.

I have always had a big fascination with books, specifically fiction books, and the library has always seemed such a magical place. It is no surprise therefore that I would want to work with books at a much closer distance and gain more insight. Besides reading, I also enjoy drawing, watching movies and visiting new places. I love swimming, in the sea and not the pool, and I spend a bit too much time in museums and galleries during trips. Mythologies in particular are a special interest of mine.

Technical Skills

Technical Skills

Prior Knowledge When it comes to my technical skills, I have never really delved into the world of programming. I can operate a computer well enough, even perform some troubleshooting, but coding and programming are something entirely new, in practice at least. During university I took a Computational Linguistics course, meaning that I can recognize most terms, such as 'markup' and 'structural units', however my paper for the class focused on semantic analysis and not on actual softwares and coding practices.
Expectations Already I feel like I am learning a lot more than what I knew beforehand. I expect that by the end of the course I will have gained a better understanding and grasp of how programming works, and how to maybe get into it myself, as well as how it applies within the sector of publishing and how it affects the current state of books. In the age of digitization there is a constant development of technologies which affects the creation, the production and the distribution of not only books, but most current media, so I look forward to how all of this can be analyzed and explored.
